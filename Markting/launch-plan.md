# Suggested seven-day distribution

These are optional editorial slots, not scheduled actions or a claim about optimal posting times. Choose hours using your audience data. Use fewer slots if several channels reach the same audience.

| Day | Copy ID | Audience and purpose | Attachment | Primary action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | LI-01; X-01 | Professional network and engineering peers: introduce the problem and portfolio | Landscape preview; attach only to first thread post | Explore portfolio |
| 2 | IG-01; FB-01 | Visual/general audience: explain the idea through the six-slide arc | Carousel 01–06; square preview for Facebook | Visit the site |
| 3 | LI-02; X-03 | Developers: explain the content architecture | Carousel 03 for LinkedIn; X text only | Read the writing |
| 4 | RD-01 **or** RD-02 | A community permitting portfolio feedback or technical discussion | Optional landscape for RD-01; RD-02 text only | Answer the specific feedback question |
| 5 | DEV-01 | Readers interested in portfolio design and Astro | Landscape cover | Explore a project after reading |
| 6 | UP-01 medium | Readers who want a concrete case-study example | Portrait preview | Give feedback on useful technical detail |
| 7 | X-02 **or** UP-01 short | A concise introduction for people who missed the earlier post | Square preview or text only | Explore one project |

HN-01 is an optional regular-submission package outside the sequence. Use one title if the topic fits the destination; there is no reason to submit three variants or solicit votes. Do not repost the same message into multiple communities just to fill a calendar.

## Ready-to-use materials

All copy IDs refer to [posts.md](posts.md). Attachments are in [images/](images/), with alternatives and descriptions in [assets.md](assets.md). The [visual gallery](index.html) makes it easy to choose exports.

## After publishing

Record the actual permalink and publication time for the posts you choose. Respond to real questions. Use observed impressions, visits, and useful conversations to decide whether a follow-up is worthwhile. Do not add invented launch results to the prepared follow-up copy.
