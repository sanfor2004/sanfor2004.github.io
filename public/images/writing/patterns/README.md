# Design pattern illustration provenance

On 12 September 2026, all 23 WebP covers were copied byte-for-byte from `public/images/learning/patterns/` for the English design-pattern blog series. The original image URLs are retained for compatibility, but the Learning pages have been replaced by redirects. New articles use `/images/writing/patterns/`.

The `diagrams/` SVG files were copied unchanged from the owner's `23-Design-Patterns/assets/diagrams/` repository. They show the C++ examples' structure; they are separate from the illustrative covers. Source code, diagrams, and adapted repository explanations retain the notice in `SOURCE-LICENSE.txt`.

These 23 cover illustrations were generated for Sanfor on 11 September 2026 with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool, then reviewed and processed locally with Sharp.

Each source prompt requested an original, wide sketchbook-style educational illustration on warm handmade paper, using dark brown ink, light watercolor, burnt-orange accents, subtle technical grid marks, and no embedded text, synthetic logos, watermarks, photorealism, or copied book diagrams. The subject of each prompt was the original real-life analogy used in its corresponding lesson:

- Abstract Factory: a workshop produces a matching furniture family from one style choice.
- Builder: ordered sandwich stations build one result step by step.
- Factory Method: a regional delivery office chooses the appropriate vehicle.
- Prototype: a prepared master form produces independent customized copies.
- Singleton: one official fire-alarm panel coordinates a building.
- Adapter: a travel plug connects incompatible electrical standards.
- Bridge: one universal remote controls several device implementations.
- Composite: nested shipping boxes contribute to one total weight.
- Decorator: successive wrapping layers add to the same gift.
- Facade: a reception desk coordinates complex hotel departments.
- Flyweight: one library edition is referenced by many borrower records.
- Proxy: a bank card controls access to a secure account.
- Chain of Responsibility: a support ticket moves through ordered handlers.
- Command: an order slip carries a request from waiter to chef.
- Interpreter: small symbol cards are combined through grammar rules.
- Iterator: a museum route exposes exhibits without revealing internals.
- Mediator: a control tower coordinates aircraft interactions.
- Memento: sealed game saves capture and restore earlier state.
- Observer: one notification reaches independent subscribers.
- State: a traffic light changes its response with its current state.
- Strategy: a traveler chooses among interchangeable routes.
- Template Method: one recipe sequence supports different chefs' steps.
- Visitor: different inspectors operate on the same building types.

The official `public/assets/brand/logo.svg` was originally composited afterward at a fixed size and with protected clear space. The initial exports were 1600 × 900 WebP assets at quality 84; original generation outputs remain outside this repository.

On 11 September 2026, the owner requested removal of the light rectangular logo background and black border. OpenAI's built-in image editing tool was used for each cover with this prompt: “Remove only the cream-white rectangular badge background and its thin black border behind the small orange logo at top-left. Restore underlying continuous warm paper/illustration texture there. Keep the orange logo unchanged in geometry, size, position, and color. Preserve the rest of the illustration.” Only the edited corner was composited back into each original, with a small feather at the perimeter. The revised covers retain their 1600 × 900 dimensions and WebP paths, exported at quality 90. The logo sits directly on the artwork without a badge.
